 Motivation 
 The Equities Hub currently holds eight tokenized equities alongside USDC. Onboarding the six tokens would: 

 Broaden the collateral on the hub with large-cap US equities whose cash markets trade between USD 1.8B and 10.9B a day. 
 Add collateral issued under the same program, token standard, and price feed design as the existing reserves, so that the market structure, liquidation routes, and oracle setup of the hub apply without change. 
 Allow borrowers on the pooled spoke to hold more diversified collateral positions, with each asset keeping its own collateral factor.

 Each of the six feeds is expected to meet these requirements, as MSTRc’s live feed does: 

 It prices the token as the underlying share price times the issuer multiplier, as on the listed reserves. 
 It publishes through the pre-market, regular, post-market and overnight sessions, from Sunday 20:00 to Friday 20:00 ET.

 The feeds stop updating from Friday 20:00 to Sunday 20:00 ET and on US market holidays, and keep showing the last price. Positions can still be liquidated in that window, but only at that price, so a move in the stock over the weekend reaches positions when the feed restarts on Sunday evening. 
 3. Perpetual Futures 
 Perpetual futures let a liquidator hedge seized collateral while the cash market is closed. All six names trade on Binance, Hyperliquid, and OKX, and all except NFLX on Lighter. Open interest is counted once per contract on every venue, so Hyperliquid’s reported figure, which sums long and short positions, is halved. 
 On November 11, 2026 open interest across the four venues ranged from USD 3.05M (NOW) to USD 42.07M (AMD) on the first five, and 24-hour volume from USD 2.64M (NOW) to USD 441.07M (AMD). MSTR carried USD 111.17M of open interest and USD 362.25M of 24-hour volume on November 12, 2026, more open interest than any Mag7 name except GOOGL and NVDA. Only AMD and MSTR exceed the smallest Mag7 total, USD 33.52M on AMZN, while the largest, NVDA, carries USD 543.06M. Binance holds the largest share of open interest on all six names. Turnover relative to open interest is highest on AMD, at about 10.5 times its open interest in a day. 
 Perpetual markets on MSTR, AMD and ORCL are deep enough to hedge a large liquidation. A quarter of a full USD 5M cap is about 1.1% of MSTR’s open interest. On PLTR, NFLX and NOW they are thinner, so a hedge of a quarter of a full cap, USD 1.25M, would move their price, especially at weekends. The hedge is not needed to protect the market: the collateral factor covers the fall until the next regular session, when seized tokens can be redeemed or sold, and the cap limits the amount at risk to USD 5M per token.

 4. The Tokens on Base

 5. Parameters 
 The six reserves join the pooled lending spoke of the Equities Hub on the same terms as the existing collateral: collateral only, a 10% liquidation fee, receive-shares enabled, and a collateral risk of 0, or 10% on MSTRc. 
 5.1 Collateral Factor 
 In Aave V4 the collateral factor is both the borrowing limit and the liquidation threshold. For a stock priced 24/5, it has to cover the largest fall from any off-hours price to the lowest price before five minutes past the next regular open, plus a 0.5% allowance for the feed’s deviation threshold. It is sized so that this fall exceeds a stock’s haircut on one night per stock in ten years, with a 2020-type crash counted as one year in ten. 
 The haircut scales a forecast of the stock’s daily volatility over the next year by the size of a once-in-ten-years off-hours fall relative to daily volatility, measured across S&P 500 stocks. The forecast weights the stock’s recent moves and pulls them partly toward the market-wide level. The collateral factor is what remains after the haircut, the maximum liquidation bonus and an allowance for interest while a position waits for liquidation, rounded down to a whole point and capped at 65%, which binds on NFLXc. At a 5.5% maximum liquidation bonus, the cap is a minimum haircut of 31.25%.

 5.2 Liquidation Bonus 
 The maximum liquidation bonus is 5.5% on the first five, the value of the Mag7 reserves on the spoke, kept until the market has a longer liquidation history. MSTRc carries 6.5%, one point more, as SPCXc does, since its weekend gaps reach positions all at once when the feed reopens. Liquidators are relied on to liquidate positions and rotate the seized tokens within about 30 minutes in the US regular session. The spoke’s bonus curve pays 90% of the maximum at a health factor of 1.0 and the full maximum from a health factor of 0.90, so the first five pay 4.95% at a health factor of 1.0 and 5.5% from 0.90, and MSTRc 5.85% and 6.5%. At a health factor just below 1.0 the liquidator keeps about 4.46% of the repaid debt after the protocol’s 10% share, 5.27% on MSTRc, against the 2.6% a hedge costs in the regular session. 
 A liquidator with redemption access, a vested holder or an authorised participant, can redeem seized tokens through the issuer during regular hours. A liquidator without it sells on Base or hedges with a perpetual short until it can sell. With secondary depth of USD 129k to 230k at a 2% price impact, liquidations beyond small sizes go through redemption or a perpetual hedge.
